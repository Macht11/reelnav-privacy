ReelNav Privacy Policy

Effective August 7, 2026

Overview

ReelNav is a browser extension that adds local video playback controls to Instagram videos and Reels.

Data collection

ReelNav does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information, browsing history, website content, authentication information, or usage data. The extension contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, account system, or developer-operated network service.

Local processing

To provide its controls, ReelNav accesses standard HTML video elements on instagram.com and changes local browser media properties such as playback time, volume, mute state, and playback speed. This processing happens only in the user's browser. Playback preferences are kept only in memory for the current page session and are not transmitted or written to persistent storage.

Permissions and site access

ReelNav runs only on https://www.instagram.com/*. This access is used solely to display and operate the video controls described in the Chrome Web Store listing. ReelNav does not access Instagram passwords, private messages, forms, cookies, or account credentials.

Third parties

ReelNav does not send data to the developer or to third parties. Instagram is a third-party website governed by its own privacy policy. ReelNav is independent and is not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by Instagram or Meta.

Children's privacy

ReelNav does not knowingly collect information from children or from any other user.

Changes to this policy

If ReelNav's data practices change, this policy and the Chrome Web Store privacy disclosures will be updated before those changes are released.
